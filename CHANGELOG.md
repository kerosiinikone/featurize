## [0.1.0] - 2026-08-30

### Added
- Initial release of featurize-core
- Compile-time-checked preprocessing pipelines
- Support for WASM and no_std environments
- Element and Transform operations
- Optional burn and candle tensor integrations
- NaN handling policies
- Image preprocessing operations (grayscale, rotation, flipping, etc.)
